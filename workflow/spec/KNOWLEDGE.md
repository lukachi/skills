# Knowledge and promotion policy

This document does not define curation or promotion policy for the redesigned bundle, unit, and flow model.

The current [optional work contract](OPTIONAL_WORK.md) establishes only that ordinary work needs no knowledge gate, that bundle and unit Markdown are durable project records, and that agent recovery notes are local and ignored. It does not settle how delivery is promoted into curated knowledge, how future reviews are represented, or how curation decisions are made. Those areas require a separate design pass.

Existing explicit knowledge and reconstruction commands remain in source during this transition. Their presence does not imply that their prior gates carry over to new work records.
