# Knowledge Note Format

Knowledge notes are topic-based indexes of accumulated research.
They must not replace, rewrite, or compress original reports.

Store notes in `research/knowledge/`.
Use relative Markdown links compatible with Obsidian.

## Template

# Topic Name

## Scope
What this knowledge covers and its boundaries.

## Findings
- Finding or conclusion.
- Evidence: [Source report](../inbox/report.md)
- Limitations or conditions, if any.

## Conflicts & Changes
- Contradictory findings with links to both sources.
- Superseded conclusions and what replaced them.
- Preserve the history of significant changes.

## Unknowns
- Unanswered questions and knowledge gaps.

## Related Research
- [Report title](../inbox/report.md) — research date, scope.
- Links to related knowledge notes when useful.

## Maintenance Rules

- Every significant finding must link to supporting reports.
- Never silently discard findings during updates.
- Preserve uncertainty and contradictory evidence.
- Update existing topic notes instead of duplicating them.
- Keep notes concise; details belong in original reports.
- Do not invent conclusions absent from source reports.
