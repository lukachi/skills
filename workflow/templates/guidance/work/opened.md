# What this work is

Two answers are the maintainer's, and this is the first of them.

**Significant or lightweight.** Significant work may change behaviour, product
meaning, contracts, data or control flow, persistent state, security,
operations, or architecture. Lightweight work is clearly local and mechanical,
and preserves both behaviour and contracts.

Put that distinction to them in your own words, in product language. Do not read
this page out, do not name a command, and do not decide it yourself — the old
workflow let the agent judge it silently, and it judged generously.

If the impact is genuinely ambiguous, say so and let them choose. Ambiguity is
not a reason to pick the heavier route on their behalf.
