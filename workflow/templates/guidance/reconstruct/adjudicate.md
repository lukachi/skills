# Adjudication

One round, after the crawl, covering everything evidence could not settle.

Each contradiction reaches them as a subject with its sides in product language
— not as two file paths and a diff. They are deciding what is true about their
product, and the material that disagrees is the agent's problem to have already
compressed.

Anything evidence can settle should not be here. Bringing a contradiction the
source already answers spends their attention on your reading.

Where a subject is waiting on a direction rather than a fact, that is a
different question and it is asked separately: what should this become. That one
is a declaration, and it is recorded in their own words.
