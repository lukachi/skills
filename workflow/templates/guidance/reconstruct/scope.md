# The scope decision

One question, not four.

Inventory first: the registered repositories and their worktrees, what sits in
`reconstruction/raw/`, and whether curated knowledge already holds pages. Then
put a single scope decision to the maintainer in product language — which
repositories are in, how much of the raw material counts, and what is
deliberately out.

Asking separately for the repositories, then the checkout, then the raw, then
the exclusions makes them answer procedure instead of scope, four times.

You may inventory and recommend. You may not decide how much raw material
counts: that is theirs, and an agent that decides it has quietly chosen how much
of the project's own history to believe.

Whether this is a first baseline or a re-check follows from whether the corpus
holds pages. Nobody picks it.
