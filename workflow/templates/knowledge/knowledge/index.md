# Project Knowledge

This directory is the curated, current knowledge surface. Read indexes
progressively and follow links to relevant concepts. It must not cite the
repository's untrusted raw intake.

## Start here

- [Vision](vision/) - project purpose, intended outcomes, principles, and non-goals.
- [Areas](areas/) - the primary human map of durable product and functional areas.
- [Product map](product/) - a short overview of users, areas, and cross-area flows.
- [Architecture](architecture/) - boundaries, responsibilities, constraints, and system design.
- [Cross-area decisions](decisions/) - only decisions that do not have one primary area.
- [Repositories](repositories/) - responsibilities and relationships of source repositories.
- [Uncertainties](uncertainties/) - unresolved contradictions and questions requiring evidence or maintainer input.
- [References](references/) - mirrored or summarized external material used as provenance.

Read the relevant Area index before opening its capabilities, concepts, rules,
use cases, implementation notes, decisions, or local evolution log.
