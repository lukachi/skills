# Implementing this unit

One slice at a time, test-first at the seams already agreed.

**Read the structure before you change it.** Follow the graph outward from what
you are touching: what calls it, what depends on it, what already does something
similar. Searching by string finds the name you guessed; searching by structure
finds what you did not know to guess. Duplicating something that already exists,
or contradicting an architecture you never read, both look identical from inside
the edit.

The counters above show what you have actually used. Many greps and no traversal
means the code was searched by string and never by structure.

**Finishing this unit is not finishing.** Completing it releases the claim and
leaves the flow holding units nobody has taken. That is the shape every long run
passes through, and the moment a turn is most likely to end on "next I will do
X" and then not. The next unit is available work, and available work is yours.

If you find a bug, a gotcha, or an improvement that is not this unit's job:
capture it. Do not open a bundle for it.
