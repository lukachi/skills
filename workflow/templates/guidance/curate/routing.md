# Which road a claim takes

Stakeholder-facing behaviour goes to the product road; technical realization
goes to the engineering road. Neither is generated from the other, and neither
may silently redefine the other's meaning.

Every page declares its view, its purpose and its audience. A product page
carries no code, identifiers, endpoints, schemas or source paths. An engineering
page links established product meaning before explaining realization, and does
not infer intent from code.

Decision lineage connects both roads rather than forming a third flat view.

[The knowledge model](../references/knowledge-model.md) carries the four routes
into curated knowledge and which gate each one answers to. The rule behind them
is one line: **a route needs the maintainer's gate exactly when it could itself
become a citable authority.**
