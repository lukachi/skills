# Third-party influences

## Matt Pocock skills

Selected project-work behavior is adapted from the MIT-licensed
[`mattpocock/skills`](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills) project, especially
its Wayfinder, specification synthesis, tracer-bullet ticketing, implementation,
test-driven development, domain-modeling, grilling, and two-axis review ideas.

The workflow does not install that suite into consumer repositories and does
not execute it as a second tracker. We periodically review a pinned upstream
revision, then deliberately integrate selected behavior into wfctl's own
skills, central knowledge bundle, worktree bindings, and deterministic gates.
Consumers receive only the reviewed integrated workflow assets.

Pinned source and review scope are recorded in
[`vendor/mattpocock/upstream.json`](vendor/mattpocock/upstream.json). The
upstream license is retained in [`vendor/mattpocock/LICENSE`](vendor/mattpocock/LICENSE).
